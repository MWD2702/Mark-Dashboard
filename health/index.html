How the benchmark is calculated

Comparable-session metrics, not a replacement for technique or RIR
Load progression = change in top working load versus the first comparable benchmark.
Rep progression = change in best reps achieved at that session's top load.
Estimated 1RM = Epley estimate from the best set: load × (1 + reps ÷ 30).
Volume = Σ(load × reps); unilateral leg work counts both sides when explicitly recorded.
Strength index = estimated 1RM ÷ first comparable benchmark × 100.
Travel handling: travel-limited sessions stay visible, but are excluded from progression KPIs when equipment clearly capped the load. Different leg/squat variants are not compared against each other.
